# Phoenix photo-funnel research notes

Research checked November 11, 2026. These are published local asking/starting prices and broad installer guides, not completed customer transactions. No cited shop is implied to be a vetted or paying partner. The executable values and notes are in `server/seeds.mjs`; exact option ranges take priority over a broader service/body-type fallback. No artificial body-size multiplier is used.

| Service | Primary sources and interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Window tint | [Phoenix tint guide](https://www.tintingsource.com/blog/window-tinting-cost-phoenix), [ShadeFX pricing](https://shadefxshop.com/pages/build-your-own), [Fast Lane price list](https://fastlanetints.com/price-list/). With no film question, side/rear bands span film tiers. The windshield-strip option adds ShadeFX's published $75 strip to the band. Windshield-only estimates use local published film pricing; scope, film and glass vary. Van coverage uses a broader larger-vehicle range. |
| Mobile detailing | [AZ Detailers](https://azdetailers.com/pricing/), [Signature Mobile](https://www.signaturemobiledetailaz.com/), [ShadeFX](https://shadefxshop.com/). Interior, exterior and full packages are recorded separately; different cleaning depth and vehicle condition affect comparability. Minivan/XL van rates are explicitly present at AZ Detailers. |
| Ceramic coating | [B&S Phoenix guide](https://bsdetailing.com/guides/how-much-does-ceramic-coating-cost-phoenix-az), [AZ Detailers](https://azdetailers.com/pricing/). Three-year uses B&S's 2–3-year band, five-plus uses its premium band. The broader AZ Detailers range is the fallback for unspecified/one-year duration and thin vehicle categories; that fallback is not a duration-specific sample. |
| Body shop | [ABD Legacy Phoenix guide](https://autobodyrepairpros.com/blog/auto-body-repair-cost-phoenix-az/), [Phoenix collision guide](https://phxautobody.com/collision-repair-cost-phoenix/). Dent/scratch, bumper, collision and repaint bands are broad; collision includes moderate through major damage. Typical is an estimate within the published range. An inspection is needed to determine actual parts, paint blending and structural scope. |
| Starlight headliner | [ShadeFX builder](https://shadefxshop.com/pages/build-your-own) and its public `pricing.js` price table. Standard is mapped to 500–900 stars; dense to 1000–1500. Shooting stars spans 500–1500 base stars plus the published $150 add-on. These density definitions are research assumptions because the deck does not specify strand counts. Suede, trim work, roof access and panoramas can add cost. |
| Oil change | [Bell Auto Spa](https://bellautospa.com/oil-lube/), [Jimmy G's](https://jimmygsautomotive.com/our-services/lube-oil-filter-change/). Separate formulation ranges use posted prices as typical anchors. Capacity, specialty filters, taxes and fees vary. The “Not sure” fallback spans the formulations; no engine capacity is inferred from body type. |
| Custom lighting | [ShadeFX builder](https://shadefxshop.com/pages/build-your-own) and [its service listing](https://www.shadefxcustoms.com/services-store/p/intermediate-service-lrnkc). Ambient packages and exterior custom LED/underglow rates are documented. Headlight upgrades fall back to the broader custom-lighting range because a broadly comparable local installed headlight price was not verified. That fallback is not represented in research records as a headlight-specific quote. |

Source notes preserve the distinctions above. The deck's requested marketing copy is used unchanged; owner review of the broad fallbacks is appropriate before substantial paid acquisition. Prior San Diego sources and records are isolated by market key and are not used by this release.
