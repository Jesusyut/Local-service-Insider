Almost everyone asks the same two things before they book. Here are straight answers.

1. Will it mess up my headliner?
No. Fiber optic strands carry light, not electricity or heat. Hair-thin fibers go through the headliner board, the light source is hidden, and nothing is drilled into your roof.

2. How many stars do I actually need?
The number is the look. 500 reads as a scattered sky, 750 looks full in most cars, and 1,000 gives you a dense galaxy, great for SUVs and bigger roofs.
