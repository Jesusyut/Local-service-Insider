Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026.

Information we collect

We store the vehicle and service details, quote amount, optional shop name, name, email, ZIP code, consent text and timestamp, and campaign attribution you submit. If you request shop quotes, we also store your phone number and timing preference. Shop names from submitted quotes are never displayed publicly.

How we use it

We use this information to provide price guidance, send your report and the newsletter you agreed to receive, evaluate local pricing, prevent abuse, and measure campaign performance. Newsletter emails include an unsubscribe link. Approved quotes contribute to aggregated pricing without your contact information.

Partner shops and lead sharing

If you separately request quotes and consent, we may share or sell your contact information and request details to the partner shops named on that request so they can call or text you. We do not share your contact details with shops merely because you run a price check or subscribe.

Service providers and measurement

When configured, Render hosts the application and database, Resend delivers reports, beehiiv manages newsletters, Cloudflare Turnstile verifies submissions, and Meta Pixel and Conversions API measure advertising events. Meta may receive campaign identifiers and hashed contact identifiers. Hashing does not make identifiers anonymous. Browser storage includes a secure owner login cookie; visitor check details remain in the current page session.

Retention and your choices

We retain submitted information while needed for these purposes and delete or deidentify it when no longer needed. To request access, correction, deletion, or opt out of future sharing, reply to a report or newsletter email. To stop marketing emails, use its unsubscribe link. To stop shop texts, reply STOP or contact the shop directly. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.

Security and changes

Owner access is restricted, traffic uses HTTPS in production, and pricing and personal records are processed on the server. No system can guarantee absolute security. We may update this policy and will post the revised effective date here.
