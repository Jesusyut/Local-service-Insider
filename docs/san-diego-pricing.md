## Sources and scope

- Tint: https://coastaltintsd.com/pricing and https://www.sbmobiledetailing.com/services/mobile-window-tint . Side/back tiers vary by body class. Full-strip references add the posted $79 visor price. Windshield references use full windshield packages. Vans use explicitly broader large-vehicle references.
- Detailing / coating: https://awesomeguys-sd.com/pricing/ , https://reflectiondetailingsd.com/pricing/ , https://www.adonisdetail.com/ceramic-coating-san-diego . Detail bands distinguish basic interior, deep cleaning, exterior detail, and complete packages. Coatings use published 1/3/5-year packages. Size S maps sedan/coupe; M maps SUV with large-SUV caveat; L maps truck/van. Some coating bounds span all sizes and are labeled broad references.
- Body: https://www.autopaintsd.com/pricing.html , https://www.autopaintsd.com/ , https://bumperfixers.com/ . Dent/scratch $150–750, bumper $350–600 (midpoint $475), repaint tier references $1,620 / $3,240 / $4,725. Premium repaint can exceed the upper reference. No structural collision estimate.
- Oil: https://www.dualtoneauto.com/oil-change . Blend $59.95, high mileage $69.95, synthetic $79.95, up to five quarts. These are one shop's package references, not market-wide fixed fees. Mechanical-work results explicitly cover oil changes only.
- Smog: https://www.montezumasmog.com/pricing . Regular inspection $49.75 for 2000+ light duty, $69.75 for older cars/pickups/vans, older trucks $89.75, plus $8.25 certificate: $58 / $78 / $98. No coupon assumed. Broader year band is used because fuel, test type and weight are not collected. No Arizona fee, state-station or universal 1996 cutoff. Requirements: https://www.bar.ca.gov/smog-check . Check DMV notice and STAR requirements; do not infer eligibility from year alone.
- Starlight: https://www.starluxautos.com/ in Lancaster, California. Explicit broader-California fallback, not San Diego research: standard 500–900 stars $625 / $775 / $925; dense 1000–1500 $1,150 / $1,350 / $1,600; shooting adds posted $175 to standard. No fabricated vehicle multiplier.
- Ambient lighting: https://interstellarinteriors.com/upgrade in Ripon, California. Explicit broader-California fallback: two-door base $995, four-door base $1,195. Extras excluded. Vans use broader reference. This does not price headlights or exterior lighting.
